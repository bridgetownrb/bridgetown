And now a word about our fundraising efforts earlier in the year.

Unfortunately, we hit a wall. We got to roughly 12% of our $400/month goal, and then…nothing.

But as I often say, you'll never get what you don't ask for. So we're back with a slightly more modest **$300/month** goal, and once again [we're asking you to sponsor Bridgetown](/sponsor). It's the only _hybrid_ progressive site generator in the Ruby ecosystem (aka you can build static sites & dynamic server-rendered pages via a unified system) and compares favorably with many web frameworks in other languages. It powers websites for Sidekiq, RuboCop, Lookbook, and other tools in the Ruby ecosystem. And we're _serious_ about protecting our community and our ethical stance by continued support for handcrafted coding enthusiasts (and blocking LLM-generated contributions).

Obviously our merch store will help support the Bridgetown project too, though we don't credit sales margin towards our sponsorship goal.

[Sponsoring Bridgetown](/sponsor) will help ensure our unique place in the web development space can keep on keeping on. Thanks to everyone who has sponsored us to date, and **here's to a bright future for Bridgetown!**
